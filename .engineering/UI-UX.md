# UI / UX — V1

## Experience principle

Nex Labs Technology should feel like an advanced research and engineering company from the first frame: dark, precise, cinematic, technically confident and continuously alive without becoming a game interface.

## Primary Home Visual Master

The owner-approved final Home composition is the N-centered living research laboratory recorded in `HOME-VISUAL-MASTER-SPEC.md`.

Primary visual hierarchy:
- selected NEX LABS identity in the header;
- large left editorial headline/copy/CTAs;
- monumental chrome Precision Blades N in a luminous cylindrical chamber on center-right;
- global-network/Earth imagery and restrained holographic panels;
- human scale cue;
- blue/cyan energy circulation across the environment;
- core-solution and lower narrative bands visually belonging to the same world.

The earlier chrome-humanoid exploration is superseded as a composition master but remains historical inspiration for human-centered technology atmosphere.

The monumental cube remains secondary visual vocabulary for Technology/Infrastructure.

## Layout rules

- Desktop art direction is asymmetric and cinematic.
- Preserve strong left editorial copy and dominant right visual world.
- Content scales fluidly rather than snapping into a generic template grid.
- Mobile adapts composition rather than shrinking desktop.
- Hero 3D is poster-first and may become STATIC.
- Cards use restrained translucent surfaces and edge light.
- Glow is an accent, not a background flood.

## Color/material direction

- near-black / blue-black / graphite fields;
- cold white primary text;
- desaturated blue-gray secondary text;
- ice cyan / electric blue energy;
- restrained violet highlight;
- silver/chrome hero material.

## Typography

- Headings: wide geometric/grotesk presence, high legibility.
- Body: neutral highly legible grotesk.
- Labels/navigation: compact with deliberate spacing.
- Final font use must remain production-license appropriate.

## Interaction feel

- smooth damped motion;
- pointer/parallax influence limited to a few degrees/pixels;
- scroll reveals depth without hijacking navigation;
- environmental energy moves continuously at low amplitude;
- no aggressive camera travel;
- no long intro loader;
- no motion that delays content.
