- The 1600×900 reference is a composed page, not a standalone hero crop. Header and Hero occupy the first ~49% of the frame (y≈0–438); the capability band occupies ~21% (y≈438–625); the Research/Technology world occupies ~24% (y≈626–840); and the footer closes the final ~7% (y≈841–900).

The center sculpture is the strongest focal object, while its surrounding chamber remains visibly larger than the sculpture.
- The capability band is a single horizontal continuation: a compact intro at left (~21% of band width) followed by five similarly sized object bays. Objects sit above short copy; they are not isolated app icons in generic cards.

## Palette by role

- **Environment:** near-black navy and graphite, with blue-black shadow regions. Avoid large flat black voids; retain faint architecture in shadow.
- **Primary structure:** cool silver/chrome with dark reflected bands, narrow white highlights, and blue edge reflections. Bright white is a reflection/highlight, not a white-painted object.
- **Energy:** electric blue and cyan used as thin rails, ring cores, signal paths and localized spill. Cyan is the navigation/action accent; it does not flood every surface.
- **Glass:** smoked blue-gray translucent planes with readable dark interiors, edge highlights and restrained information traces.
- **Atmosphere:** low-contrast blue haze and sparse fine particles. The light source remains legible through depth; do not add generic particle bursts.
- **Text:** high-contrast white for the main headline, softened cool-white for paragraphs, and tracked cool-gray uppercase for eyebrow labels.

## Materials and lighting

- Chrome needs a dark environment response, broad cool softboxes, thin white specular bands and electric-blue edge light. Preserve bevels and thickness at screenshot scale; do not render the N as a flat white cutout.
- The chamber is cylindrical: curved ribs, repeated vertical bays, upper and lower concentric rings, radial supports and partially occluded side structures establish enclosure. Lines should overlap and disappear behind nearer structures to imply depth.
- The platform is a dark metallic floor with nested circular plates, ring seams, narrow emissive inlays and controlled reflections. No broad pale plane.
- Holographic panels use multiple smoked-glass layers, visible side thickness, luminous frame edges and restrained abstract traces. No fake readable dashboard, data claim or label.
- Use a cold overhead key, a brighter central axial source, side blue/cyan rim sources and lower-floor bounce. The N receives reflected chamber light and should appear physically embedded in the room.

## Density and spatial layering

- **Foreground:** floor seams, near rails, occasional console edges, controlled reflection and one small human-scale cue.
- **Midground:** N platform, N, Earth/network, glass panels and lateral consoles.
- **Background:** curved chamber shell, vertical ribs, ring structures, remote instrumentation and low-contrast energy paths.
- Every major scene region should contain at least two depth cues: overlap/occlusion, perspective convergence, scale falloff, atmospheric contrast, reflection or parallax. Density is architectural and organized, not a larger glow budget.
- Keep a purposeful quiet pocket behind the headline for readability, but continue faint structures through it so it does not become empty black space.

## Edge, type and motion

- Major structural edges are machined, beveled or double-layered. HUD edges are thin and luminous; structural edges stay predominantly dark chrome.
- Headline is a large geometric display face with compact line spacing and strong left alignment. Body copy is a neutral interface sans. Eyebrows use small uppercase letters with generous tracking. Keep the existing canonical wording.
- Motion implies circulation through the room: slow ring energy, a subtle network pulse, purposeful panel parallax and low-frequency light travel. No continuous N spin, indiscriminate fade-in, large camera travel or random motion.
- Reduced motion freezes nonessential scene motion and retains the same object hierarchy and readable poster-first composition.

## Capability-object vocabulary

1. AI: dense, branching neural/brain lattice with clustered nodes.
2. Infrastructure: floating stacked plates with visible gaps and light between layers.
3. Interfaces: network globe with a precise geodesic shell and orbital signal paths.
4. Sustainable technologies: dimensional energy torus, not a flat ring glyph.
5. Research platforms: faceted crystal with internal refraction and metallic edges.

Each object must remain recognizable in a compact card and use a consistent cold chrome/cyan material language without repeating one silhouette five times.

## Section transitions

The Hero chamber flows into capabilities through continuing perspective rails and blue energy. Research inherits the same laboratory, Earth/network scale and panel grammar. Technology inherits the floor/platform and layered illuminated stack. Footer rules, separators and chrome details close the same world instead of starting a new template.

## Banned patterns

- Hero as a sparse wireframe diagram or a centered object on an empty gradient.
- Large pale floor, flat icon-only capability row, generic rounded-card grid, or glow used in place of form.
- Bright cyan everywhere, gaming HUD overload, generic particle explosions, oversized blank panels, fake metrics/search/social links, new claims, or any change to approved copy/brand geometry.
- Any master screenshot, crop, traced pixel texture or derivative used in production runtime.
