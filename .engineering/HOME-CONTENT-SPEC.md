# Home Content Specification — M05

Status: CANONICAL M05 CONTENT SOURCE

## Content principles

- English remains the canonical website language for V1.
- Copy must be concise, premium and technology-forward.
- Do not publish invented proof, numbers, client logos, partner claims, awards or testimonials.
- Positioning statements describe intent, focus and engineering approach, not third-party validation.

## Core Capabilities

Eyebrow: `OUR CORE CAPABILITIES`

Heading: `Intelligence in action.`

Intro: `Nex Labs explores intelligent systems that connect software, data and real-world environments through focused research and engineering.`

1. **Artificial Intelligence** — `AI-native systems designed around useful reasoning, automation and human-centered workflows.`
2. **Intelligent Infrastructure** — `Software and data foundations designed to support reliable, observable and adaptable intelligent systems.`
3. **Advanced Interfaces** — `Interfaces that make complex systems easier to understand, operate and collaborate with.`
4. **Sustainable Technologies** — `Technology concepts shaped by efficiency, responsible resource use and long-term operational thinking.`
5. **Research Platforms** — `Experimental environments for turning technical questions into testable systems and measurable learning.`

Section CTA: `Explore the system` → `#infrastructure`.

## Principles

Eyebrow: `HOW WE BUILD`

Use exactly these four non-numeric principles:

- **Human-centered** — `Technology should expand what people can understand, create and operate.`
- **Research-led** — `Important decisions should be shaped by evidence, experiments and measurable feedback.`
- **Secure by design** — `Security, privacy and failure containment belong in the architecture from the beginning.`
- **Built for real-world systems** — `Ideas become valuable when they survive the constraints of actual users, hardware and operations.`

Do not render counters or pseudo-statistics.
