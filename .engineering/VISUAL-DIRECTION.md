# Visual Direction — Nex Labs Technology Website V1

Status: CANONICAL PLANNING SOURCE for M01.

## Selected concept

The owner selected the chrome-humanoid Home concept shown in the design exploration on 2026-10-02 as the primary visual target. The website is expected to reproduce its composition and atmosphere with high fidelity, not simply use it as loose inspiration.

Key identity:
- dark/near-black cinematic environment;
- chrome and glass intelligence figure as the dominant hero object;
- cold white, cyan, blue and restrained violet highlights;
- clean editorial headline on the left;
- thin navigation;
- glass capability cards;
- sparse, precise HUD/detailing;
- premium technology-lab feeling;
- large areas of negative space;
- fluid, controlled depth and motion.

## Secondary concept

The floating monumental cube concept is also approved as a secondary visual vocabulary for Technology/Infrastructure sections.

## 3D behavior

Hero object behavior should include:
- subtle idle movement rather than constant rotation;
- small cursor/camera parallax;
- traveling light/reflection accents;
- restrained particle/orbit depth cues;
- scroll-driven reveals/transitions;
- graceful transition from poster to live scene;
- no motion required for reading or navigation.

## Visual anti-patterns

Do not:
- turn the site into a generic cyberpunk/gaming dashboard;
- use excessive neon or blur;
- overload the page with HUD elements;
- animate every component;
- introduce fake social proof;
- sacrifice text contrast to visual effects;
- let the 3D canvas dominate mobile usability.
