# UI / UX — V1

## Experience principle

Nex Labs Technology should feel like an advanced research and engineering company from the first frame: dark, precise, cinematic and technically confident. The interface should be visually memorable without sacrificing speed, legibility or control.

## Visual Master: Chrome Intelligence

The owner selected the chrome humanoid "Where Intelligence Flows" homepage concept as the primary reference.

Core composition:
- Near-black space/technical environment.
- Large left-aligned editorial hero headline.
- Dominant chrome/holographic human intelligence figure occupying the right half.
- Sparse cyan/ice-blue/violet illumination.
- Thin glass panels and restrained HUD elements.
- Four capability cards immediately below the hero.
- Strong negative space and thin geometric typography.
- Lower narrative section with a cinematic technology environment.
- Premium, low-noise appearance; avoid crowded cyberpunk treatment.

The second approved floating-cube concept is reserved for Technology/Infrastructure storytelling.

## Layout rules

- Desktop art direction is intentionally asymmetric and cinematic.
- Content container and typography must scale fluidly rather than snapping into a generic grid.
- Mobile adapts composition, not merely shrinks it. Hero 3D may become poster-first or static.
- Cards use subtle translucent surfaces, border highlights and depth, never excessive blur.
- Glow is an accent, not a background flood.

## Color direction

Initial token direction:
- background-0: near black.
- background-1: blue-black / graphite.
- text-primary: cold white.
- text-secondary: desaturated blue-gray.
- accent-cyan: ice/cyan.
- accent-blue: electric blue.
- accent-violet: restrained violet.
- chrome highlights: neutral/silver.

Exact values are implementation tokens and require visual comparison against the master.

## Typography

- Headings: geometric, modern, light-to-medium weight, wide visual presence.
- Body: highly legible neutral grotesk.
- Navigation and labels: compact uppercase or title case with deliberate letter spacing.
- Final font families require licensing/production verification during M02; visual proportions take precedence over brand-name font selection.

## Logo direction

Create an original N monogram formed from 2–3 interlocking blades/ribbons/planes.
Requirements:
- recognizable at favicon size;
- strong monochrome silhouette;
- works in flat SVG;
- premium chrome/glass presentation can exist as an enhanced variant;
- subtle assemble/light-pass idle sequence is acceptable;
- hover movement is small and returns to rest;
- reduced-motion uses the static mark.

## Interaction feel

- Smooth, damped motion.
- Cursor/parallax influence limited to a few degrees.
- Scroll choreography reveals depth without hijacking navigation.
- Avoid spinning objects, aggressive camera movement, long intro loaders and motion that delays content.
