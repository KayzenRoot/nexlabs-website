# Innovation Ledger

## Active V1 product innovations

### I-0001 — Capability-aware cinematic 3D hero

Status: ADMITTED FOR PLANNING

The Home hero uses one visual identity across FULL, BALANCED and STATIC quality tiers rather than treating fallback as a separate inferior page. A poster renders immediately and live 3D replaces it only when capability and readiness permit.

Evidence/validation required:
- visual continuity across tiers;
- reduced-motion compliance;
- runtime/transfer-size benchmarks;
- fallback behavior when canvas or model loading fails.

### I-0002 — Vector-first living N monogram

Status: ADMITTED FOR PLANNING

The brand mark remains a robust static SVG at its core, with an optional subtle assemble/light-pass motion layer. The enhanced version must never be required for recognition or navigation.

Final asset selection and originality review remain future M03 work.
