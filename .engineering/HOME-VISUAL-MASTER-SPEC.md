## Living-organism motion language

Motion behaves like circulation in one machine:
- blue energy travels continuously through long curves/filaments;
- light pulses propagate through chamber rails and platform rings;
- holographic globe/network slowly rotates or evolves;
- particles drift with low amplitude and depth;
- camera gently breathes rather than flies;
- pointer changes parallax and local light response, not camera orientation dramatically;
- scroll changes depth/energy emphasis and hands the visual flow to later sections;
- the selected N never continuously spins.

## Responsive adaptation

At mobile width, fidelity means preserving hierarchy and atmosphere, not squeezing the desktop canvas into a phone:
- semantic copy first;
- N/chamber poster or reduced scene second;
- simplified energy language;
- no tiny HUD panels;
- no wide card row in M04;
- no horizontal scroll.
