# ADR-0002 — Defer Product Stack Selection

Status: ACCEPTED

## Context

The current Work Order is exclusively bootstrap/governance and explicitly prohibits frontend implementation.

## Decision

Defer selection of the website framework, rendering/runtime, content system, design system, logo, and deployment platform to a future product Work Order.

## Consequences

No product dependency, page, component, logo, or deployment configuration is admitted by the bootstrap increment. Future choices require explicit requirements and a recorded decision.
