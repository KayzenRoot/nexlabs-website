# Visual Direction — Nex Labs Technology Website V1

Status: CANONICAL PLANNING SOURCE for M01.

## Selected concept

The owner selected the chrome-humanoid Home concept shown in the design exploration on 2026-10-02 as the primary visual target. The website is expected to reproduce its composition and atmosphere with high fidelity, not simply use it as loose inspiration.

Key identity:
- dark/near-black cinematic environment;
- chrome and glass intelligence figure as the dominant hero object;
- cold white, cyan, blue and restrained violet highlights;
- clean editorial headline on the left;
- thin navigation;
- glass capability cards;
- sparse, precise HUD/detailing;
- premium technology-lab feeling;
- large areas of negative space;
- fluid, controlled depth and motion.

## Secondary concept

The floating monumental cube concept is also approved as a secondary visual vocabulary for Technology/Infrastructure sections.

## 3D behavior

Hero object behavior should include:
- subtle idle movement rather than constant rotation;
- small cursor/camera parallax;
- traveling light/reflection accents;
- restrained particle/orbit depth cues;
- scroll-driven reveals/transitions;
- graceful transition from poster to live scene;
- no motion required for reading or navigation.

## Visual anti-patterns

Do not:
- turn the site into a generic cyberpunk/gaming dashboard;
- use excessive neon or blur;
- overload the page with HUD elements;
- animate every component;
- introduce fake social proof;
- sacrifice text contrast to visual effects;
- let the 3D canvas dominate mobile usability.

## Final owner-approved Home composition

The owner subsequently approved a more specific Home composition derived from the selected identity exploration. This is now the primary implementation target for the Home visual world.

Required composition:
- header with the selected NEX LABS identity on the left and restrained navigation;
- cinematic hero with large left editorial headline and supporting copy;
- dominant chrome N at center-right inside a luminous laboratory/portal chamber;
- human figure in the environment for scale;
- Earth / global-network imagery integrated into the laboratory world;
- electric cyan/blue energy filaments that visually connect hero, cards and lower sections;
- core-solution cards directly below the hero;
- research/breakthroughs and technology-platform sections that remain visually inside the same world;
- dark near-black field, chrome, cold white, cyan/electric blue and restrained violet;
- strong depth, negative space and precision.

### Living-organism behavior

The page should feel alive without behaving like a game UI.

Use:
- slow travelling light along structural edges and the selected N;
- subtle energy/network filaments with independent low-amplitude motion;
- restrained particles and holographic depth;
- pointer parallax measured in a few degrees/pixels;
- scroll-linked depth/reveal choreography;
- small reactive illumination on cards/buttons;
- environmental motion that continues gently while the user is idle;
- smooth transitions between visual sections so the page reads as one connected organism.

Do not:
- run constant logo spins;
- use aggressive camera travel;
- saturate every surface with animation;
- let motion delay semantic content or interaction;
- violate reduced-motion or STATIC tier behavior.

The implementation should be judged for fidelity to this composition, not merely for sharing a blue futuristic color palette.
