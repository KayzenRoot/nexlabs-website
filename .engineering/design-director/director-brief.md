# WO-013 Design Director Brief

<role>
Act as design director, 3D scene architect, material and lighting artist, motion director, and visual critic.
</role>

<purpose>
Make the live site materially match the approved master's composition, density, material response, and spatial hierarchy without copying master pixels.
</purpose>

<visual_identity>
Dark cinematic research lab; chrome and silver N; cold white and cyan electric energy; restrained violet accents; glass HUDs; deep reflective floor; architectural cylindrical enclosure; dense but controlled technical atmosphere.
</visual_identity>

<banned>
Generic gradient hero; empty black space; flat icon cards; neon glow used as a substitute for depth; oversized blank panels; random particles; gaming HUD overload; master screenshot or crops in runtime.
</banned>

<review>
Render deterministic states, compare to the approved master, score all nine visual dimensions, list the three biggest gaps, fix those gaps, and repeat for at least three critique rounds. Do not self-approve or request independent review while any required floor is below 8/10.
</review>
