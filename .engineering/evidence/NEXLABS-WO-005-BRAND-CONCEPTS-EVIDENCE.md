# Evidence Bundle — NEXLABS-WO-005-BRAND-CONCEPTS

## Owner selection

The owner explicitly selected the **Precision Blades / Finalist A** N-monogram direction during the governed visual exploration.

Selected concept ID: `NEX-N-A-PRECISION-BLADES`

Selected identity characteristics:
- strong readable capital-N silhouette;
- two metallic/chrome blade planes with a dominant diagonal bridge;
- restrained electric-blue edge/light accent;
- premium technical feel;
- usable as symbol-only and with the NEX LABS / TECHNOLOGY lockup;
- flat monochrome form remains mandatory as the identity source of truth;
- enhanced chrome/glass presentation is a treatment, not the identity geometry.

## Website visual master selection

The owner also approved the final Home direction as the reference that the implementation should reproduce as faithfully as practical.

Canonical visual characteristics:
- dark near-black / blue-black technology laboratory;
- large chrome N presented as the primary hero object inside a luminous cylindrical/portal-like chamber;
- human figure for scale and human-centered narrative;
- Earth / orbital / connected-world imagery;
- electric-blue/cyan energy trails and network filaments;
- restrained violet accents;
- information panels and holographic displays used as atmospheric depth, not UI clutter;
- hero copy on the left, cinematic object/environment on the right;
- modular cards below the hero for intelligence, infrastructure, interfaces, sustainable technologies and research;
- research and technology-platform sections continue the same world rather than looking like separate templates;
- header and footer use the selected N identity;
- overall website must behave as a **living organism**: subtle continuous energy flow, light movement, depth, parallax and reactive motion while preserving readability and performance.

The selected visual reference exists in the current design conversation. Pixel-level implementation review requires importing a compressed reference copy into the implementation branch or otherwise making the exact approved image available to the executor/reviewer.

## Concept exploration evidence

The exploration produced more than the required 12 directions and covered the four canonical families:
- precision blades;
- ribbon / flow;
- crystalline / glass;
- monolithic / architectural.

The owner selected Precision Blades / Finalist A after reviewing the concept boards and identity applications.

## Rejected directions

Ribbon/flow variants were not selected as the primary mark because the owner preferred the stronger technical/architectural silhouette of Finalist A.

Crystalline/glass variants remain useful as supporting visual vocabulary, but not as the primary identity.

Monolithic variants remain a valid secondary design language for technology/infrastructure visuals, but not the selected brand mark.
